\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Testbed, not production.}
The deployment model follows documented framework and product behaviour (\cref{tab:grounding}), but
every number comes from our pipeline. A deployment with different cache scoping, memory design or
sync schedule will see different values; the per-artifact attribution and the lifetime structure are
what we expect to transfer.

\paragraph{Corpora.}
The generated corpus is regular by construction; the Enron corpus is real text with synthetic tenancy
and ACLs, and its facts are spans of benchmark answers. 457 of the 500 restricted Enron e-mails have no
same-thread sibling, so on Enron the sibling/singleton split of the existence results is effectively a
split by corpus. Neither has the scale of an enterprise
collection.

\paragraph{One retrieval stack.}
Every run uses the same MiniLM encoder and cross-encoder; cache thresholds are properties of that
embedding space.

\paragraph{Judging.}
Exact matching of corpus-unique values misses paraphrased disclosure. \pending{If labelled: the
model judge's recall, measured on N hand-labelled responses, bounds how much it misses. Otherwise:
the model judge is not validated, so we cannot bound how much exact matching misses.}

\paragraph{Attacker strength.}
The existence attacker is gradient boosting on hand-designed features with four fixed probes, trained
on shadow tenants. An adaptive attacker that repeats probes, or one with unlimited queries, may do
better, so chance-level results are statements about this attacker.

\paragraph{Timing.}
Time between probes is simulated; generation latency is measured per call on one machine and network
jitter is not modelled beyond 1\,ms rounding, which makes our timing results optimistic for the
attacker relative to a remote client.

\paragraph{Scope of the defenses.}
They cover the index, the cache and chat memory. Logs, exports, fine-tuning data and tool outputs
(assumption A2) are out of scope, and no artifact-level mechanism stops a user who paraphrases what
it once read (A1).
