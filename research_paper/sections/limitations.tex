\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Testbed, not production.}
The deployment model follows documented framework and product behaviour (\cref{tab:grounding}), but
every number except the real-stack check (\cref{sec:res-realstack}, 10 targets, two generators) comes from
our pipeline. A deployment with different cache scoping, memory design or sync schedule will see
different values; the per-artifact attribution and the lifetime structure are what we expect to
transfer.

\paragraph{One model, small samples.}
The model run uses one model, Llama-3.1-8B in its default 4-bit build, with 100 revocation events per
cell; larger and hosted models are not measured, and quantization may change what the model repeats.
Intervals are correspondingly wide, and the broader defense comparison, the existence experiment and
the update experiment use the stub generator only.

\paragraph{Rewordings.}
The model run's fresh-session probes use rewordings written by the generator model itself, which may
vary less than real users' wording. The stub runs repeat questions verbatim, so their cache leakage
holds for the repeated question only.

\paragraph{Corpus.}
The Enron text, questions and answers are real, but tenancy and permissions are derived from each
message's recipients, not taken from a real access-control system, and facts are spans of benchmark
answers. 457 of the 500 restricted Enron e-mails have no same-thread sibling, so few existence targets
have a hard negative in the twin world. The corpus does not have the scale of an enterprise
collection.

\paragraph{One retrieval stack.}
Every run uses the same MiniLM encoder and cross-encoder; cache thresholds are properties of that
embedding space.

\paragraph{Judging.}
Exact matching of corpus-unique values misses paraphrased disclosure. The model judge is not
validated against human labels, so we cannot bound how much exact matching misses.

\paragraph{Attacker strength.}
The existence attacker is gradient boosting on hand-designed features with four fixed probes, trained
on shadow tenants. An adaptive attacker that repeats probes, or one with unlimited queries, may do
better, so chance-level results are statements about this attacker.

\paragraph{Timing.}
Time between probes is simulated, and the existence experiment's generation latency comes from the
stub's latency model, so its timing results say nothing about a served model.

\paragraph{Scope of the defenses.}
They cover the index, the cache and chat memory. Logs, exports, fine-tuning data and tool outputs
(assumption A2) are out of scope, and no artifact-level mechanism stops a user who paraphrases what
it once read (A1).
