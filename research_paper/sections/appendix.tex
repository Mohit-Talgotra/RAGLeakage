\section{Pilot study (logs of 7--12 October 2026)}
\label{app:pilot}

This appendix reports a pilot, not the paper's results. Before the final runs we re-analysed the
logs of an earlier pilot (one generated corpus of 500
documents, 51 words per restricted document, one chunk each; stub, Qwen2.5-3B and Llama-3.1-8B;
the deployment's cache shared across each tenant) with the judge and statistics of
\cref{sec:method}. Four results changed the design.

\paragraph{The judge.}
The pilot's analysis, which excluded a value only if it appeared in the response's own source
documents, reported one leaking event under the full defense with Llama-3.1-8B and one with the stub. Both
were values shared with other documents: a city name that the model repeated while saying the
document did not mention it, and a hazard ratio that another project's report also contained. With
corpus-unique judging, the full defense leaks 0.000 in all 100 events with each generator. 21\% of
the pilot corpus's fact values and 43\% of Enron's recur in another document.

\paragraph{Most stub leakage is re-disclosure.}
Under the pilot's \texttt{baseline}, the stub disclosed $\LM=0.585$ [0.526, 0.641] (250 events, 124
targets), of which $\LM^{\text{new}}=0.058$ [0.025, 0.098] was new to the victim; the warm-up had
already shown it 66\% of the target's secrets. Real models differ: Qwen2.5-3B disclosed 0.694 [0.649,
0.733] with $\LM^{\text{new}}=0.358$ [0.306, 0.409], and Llama-3.1-8B 0.506 [0.458, 0.551] with
$\LM^{\text{new}}=0.145$ [0.109, 0.183] (100 events each). This motivated the decomposition, the probe
about unseen facts, the post-revocation update, and real models as primary evidence.

\paragraph{The existence signal of the pilot baseline was a shared cache.}
Insiders reached AUC 0.714 [0.664, 0.765] and outsiders 0.512 [0.490, 0.532]; the content channel
alone gave insiders 0.717. Cached answers generated for an authorized colleague were served to
insiders without access. With the full defense the combined AUC was 0.495 [0.486, 0.504] with the
stub and 0.540 [0.520, 0.560] with Llama-3.1-8B, whose latency was replayed from a generation cache;
the existence results of this paper use the stub.

\paragraph{Identifier purges and the stale index.}
The pilot's \texttt{legacy} preset matched \texttt{baseline} in total leakage (0.585 against 0.585)
because both keep the stale index, which dominates, while \texttt{legacy} did lower memory leakage
(0.230 against 0.322). Total leakage therefore cannot show that identifier purges miss summaries; an
ablation with the live ACL check in place would (future work).
