MERIDIAN CORP — STRATEGIC ACQUISITION TARGET ANALYSIS

Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — C-Suite & Board Only

STRATEGIC RATIONALE
To support our 2028 revenue targets post-MRD-441 launch, the Corporate Development
team has identified three priority acquisition targets to expand our oncology pipeline
and strengthen the autoimmune franchise.

TARGET A — CODENAME "FALCON" (DO NOT DISCLOSE)
  Company:   Privata Therapeutics (private, Series D)

  Focus:     KRAS G12C inhibitors — best-in-class differentiation
  Est. valuation: $1.4–1.7B
  Status:    Confidential NDA signed; preliminary term sheet under review
  Rationale: Complements MRD-441; positions Meridian in NSCLC combo therapy space

TARGET B — CODENAME "OSPREY"
  Company:   Genova Biosciences (private, Series C)

  Rationale: Platform technology extends autoimmune pipeline by 3–5 assets

TARGET C — CODENAME "HARRIER"
  Company:   Zynecor Ltd (LSE: ZYN)

  Focus:     Bispecific antibodies for haematologic malignancies
  Market cap: ~$890M (as of 9 Oct 2025)
  Status:    Monitoring; no formal contact initiated
  Rationale: EU commercial footprint, existing EU EMA relationship
