PROJECT NIGHTINGALE — PHASE III CLINICAL TRIAL INTERIM REPORT
Drug: MRD-441 (nextomazumab) | Indication: Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — Medical Affairs & Regulatory Only
Prepared: January 2026

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Project Nightingale is Meridian Corp's Phase III registrational trial for MRD-441
(nextomazumab), a fully human anti-PD-L2 monoclonal antibody, in patients with
previously treated, PD-L2-high non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

Trial ID:    NCT-2024-MRDX-441-III
Sites:       14 academic medical centres (US: 8, EU: 4, APAC: 2)
Enrollment:  847 patients (424 MRD-441 arm; 423 placebo + SOC arm)
Period:      March 2024 – November 2025

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KEY EFFICACY FINDINGS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRIMARY ENDPOINT: Progression-Free Survival (PFS) at 18 months
  MRD-441 arm:  67.3%
  Placebo arm:  41.2%
  Hazard ratio: 0.51 (95% CI: 0.42–0.62)
  p-value:      < 0.0001  ← Highly statistically significant

SECONDARY ENDPOINTS
  Overall Response Rate (ORR):
    MRD-441: 54.8%  |  Placebo: 22.1%
  Median Duration of Response: 14.6 months (MRD-441) vs 7.3 months (placebo)
  Overall Survival (OS) at 18 months (immature, follow-up ongoing):
    MRD-441: 74.1%  |  Placebo: 61.8%  (HR 0.72; p = 0.048)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SAFETY PROFILE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Any-grade treatment-related adverse events: 82.4%
  Grade 3–4 adverse events:                  18.4%
  Treatment discontinuation due to AEs:        6.7%
  Fatal treatment-related events:              1.2% (5 patients; all adjudicated as
                                               consistent with known class effects)

  Most common Grade ≥ 3 events: pneumonitis (4.2%), fatigue (3.8%), colitis (2.9%)
  No unexpected safety signals. Profile consistent with approved PD-1/PD-L1 agents.
