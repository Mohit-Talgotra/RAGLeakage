MERIDIAN CORP — PRODUCT ROADMAP UPDATE
Period: Q1–Q2 2026 | Classification: Internal — General Distribution

STRATEGIC THEMES FOR 2026
1. Regulatory submissions: Two NDA/BLA filings targeted in H1 2026.
2. Pipeline acceleration: Initiate two new Phase I programs by end of Q2.
3. Digital infrastructure: Complete migration of clinical data systems to GCP.

KEY MILESTONES (PUBLIC-SAFE SUMMARY)
Q1 2026
  - Submit IND for MRD-502 (next-gen TGF-β inhibitor)
  - Complete interim analysis for MRD-210 Phase II cohort B
  - Launch new patient-support portal (MyMeridian)

Q2 2026
  - BLA submission for lead oncology asset (Phase III program)
  - Initiate MRD-317 Phase IIb enrollment
  - Present data at ASCO Annual Meeting

TECHNOLOGY INITIATIVES
The Digital Medicine team is piloting a conversational AI assistant ("MeridianMind") for
internal knowledge retrieval. This assistant connects to the company knowledge base and
assists employees with clinical, regulatory, and operational queries.

RESOURCE ALLOCATION
R&D spend is guided toward the oncology franchise (55%), autoimmune (30%), and
early discovery (15%). Headcount growth of ~200 roles is planned across
clinical operations, regulatory affairs, and software engineering.
