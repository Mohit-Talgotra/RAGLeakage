VOLTAIC SYSTEMS ENGINEERING BLOG
Title: How We Achieve Sub-10ms Dispatch Latency at the Grid Edge
Author: Engineering Team | Published: November 2025

INTRODUCTION
When a grid operator signals a frequency deviation, every millisecond counts.
Traditional cloud-round-trip architectures introduce 80–200ms of latency — far
too slow for primary frequency response markets that require action within 2 seconds.
Voltaic's EdgePulse architecture solves this by pushing inference to the substation.

ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
The EdgePulse stack runs on ruggedised edge compute nodes (Voltaic EPN-400 series)
co-located at each substation. Each node runs:
  - VoltGrid OS v4.x (real-time OS with deterministic scheduling)
  - A quantised TensorFlow Lite model for load-forecasting & dispatch optimisation
  - A local CAN-bus interface to the smart inverter fleet

BENCHMARK RESULTS (Q3 2025, ERCOT pilot)
  Median dispatch-decision latency:  6.4 ms
  99th-percentile latency:          11.2 ms
  Model re-training cycle:          Every 4 hours (federated, privacy-preserving)
  Uptime (rolling 90 days):         99.97%

LESSONS LEARNED
1. Model quantisation is non-negotiable: INT8 reduced inference time by 4× vs FP32.
2. Deterministic scheduling (PREEMPT_RT kernel patch) eliminated jitter spikes.
3. Federated learning lets us improve the global model without centralising raw data.

NEXT STEPS
We are extending EdgePulse to support multi-site co-optimisation — coordinating
dispatch across 50+ substations to participate in wholesale energy markets as a
virtual power plant (VPP). Early results (Austin metro pilot) show a 12% increase
in revenue per asset compared to single-site optimisation.
