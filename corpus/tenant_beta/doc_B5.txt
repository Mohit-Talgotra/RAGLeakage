VOLTAIC SYSTEMS — CUSTOMER DATA HANDLING POLICY
Version: 3.0 | Effective: 1 January 2026
Classification: CONFIDENTIAL — Legal, Compliance & Engineering Leads Only

1. SCOPE
This policy governs collection, storage, processing, and sharing of all data
received from Voltaic Systems utility customers under active MSA agreements.

2. CUSTOMER DATA CATEGORIES

  Category A — Operational Telemetry (e.g., real-time power flow, device status)
    Retention: 24 months online (BigQuery), 7 years cold (GCS Nearline)
    Access: Grid Operations team + customer-designated admin accounts only

  Category B — Customer PII (e.g., end-user energy-usage records linked to addresses)
    Retention: 12 months online, 5 years cold per CPUC/FERC requirements
    Access: Restricted to Privacy Office + legal holds only; no engineering access
    Encryption: AES-256 at rest; TLS 1.3 in transit; keys held in Cloud HSM

  Category C — Model Training Data (anonymised telemetry used in FL rounds)
    Retention: Per federated learning schedule; raw tensors deleted after aggregation
    Access: AI Platform team only; logged and audited quarterly

3. DATA SHARING RESTRICTIONS
Customer data SHALL NOT be shared with:
  - Any third party not named in the applicable MSA Schedule B
  - Internal teams outside the minimum-access principle defined in §4
  - AI/ML training pipelines unless data has passed Category C anonymisation review

5. BREACH NOTIFICATION
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the CISO within 1 hour of discovery.
Customer notification SLA: 72 hours per GDPR Art. 33 / CCPA § 1798.82.
