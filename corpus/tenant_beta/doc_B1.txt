VOLTAIC SYSTEMS — COMPANY OVERVIEW
Founded: 2014 | Headquarters: Austin, TX | Employees: ~850

Voltaic Systems is a B2B energy-technology company delivering grid-edge intelligence
software and distributed energy resource (DER) management platforms to utilities,
independent power producers, and commercial & industrial customers.

CORE PRODUCTS
  VoltGrid OS     — Real-time SCADA & energy management system (EMS)
  FlexDispatch    — AI-driven demand-response and load-shifting platform
  StoreIQ         — Battery energy storage system (BESS) optimisation suite

MARKETS SERVED
  Investor-owned utilities (12 utility customers, covering ~18M endpoints)
  Renewable project developers (solar + wind + BESS hybrid sites)
  Large commercial & industrial (data centres, cold-chain logistics, manufacturing)

TECHNOLOGY DIFFERENTIATORS
Voltaic's patent-pending EdgePulse architecture runs inference at the substation
level, reducing round-trip latency to <8 ms for real-time dispatch decisions —
critical for frequency regulation markets (CAISO, ERCOT, PJM).

PARTNERSHIPS
  Hardware integrations: Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens Energy
  Cloud infrastructure: Google Cloud (primary), AWS (DR)
  Academic partners: UT Austin GridLab, NREL
